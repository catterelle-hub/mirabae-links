The questions you are already asking.

I have tried everything. Why would this be different?

Most plans work on what you eat. This works one step earlier, on the moment the urge shows up. That is why the plans that ignored the signal did not hold.

Is something wrong with me?

No. Thinking about food all day, losing control after a few bites, eating more at night after eating very little during the day: many people know these patterns. They are signals, not a character flaw. If they cause you real distress, please talk to a doctor or a qualified professional.

Is this a diet?

No. No calorie counting, no banned foods, no weigh-ins. Regulation, not restriction. If you are actually hungry, the app tells you to eat.

Can sixty seconds really change anything?

Sixty seconds will not fix everything. It gives you a gap between the urge and the action, and in that gap you get to choose. Try it above and see what your number does.

Is this medical treatment?

No. It is a self-reflection tool. If thoughts about food cause you real distress, or you recognise signs of an eating disorder, please talk to a doctor or a qualified professional.
